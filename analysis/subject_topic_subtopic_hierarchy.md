# GPSC Syllabus: Subject → Topic → Sub-topic Hierarchy

## History and Culture

- **Topic 1:** Indus valley civilisation, Vedic age, Jainism and Buddhism
- **Topic 2:** Contact with Central Asia and its consequences
- **Topic 3:** Major dynasties of Ancient, Medieval and Modern India and Gujarat
- **Topic 4:** Bhakti Movement and Sufism
- **Topic 5:** Arrival of Europeans, Company rule, British expansion, 1857, reforms, freedom movement, revolutionaries
- **Topic 6:** National leaders and their contributions
- **Topic 7:** Post-Independence India and Gujarat
- **Topic 8:** Cultural heritage: art, literature, sculpture, architecture, music, dance
- **Topic 9:** Saintly traditions and influence
- **Topic 10:** Life traditions, fairs, festivals, food, costumes
- **Topic 11:** Museums, libraries, Gujarati theatre traditions
- **Topic 12:** Adivasi life and traditions
- **Topic 13:** Gujarati literature, language and dialects
- **Topic 14:** Pilgrimage and tourist places of Gujarat

## Constitution, Polity, Social Justice and International Relations

- **Topic 1:** Indian Constitution: origin, evolution, features, amendments, basic structure, federalism
- **Topic 2:** Constitutional, statutory, regulatory and quasi-judicial bodies
- **Topic 3:** Local self-government institutions
- **Topic 4:** Judiciary: structure, functions, judicial review, PIL, landmark judgments
- **Topic 5:** Foreign policy, international relations, institutions, agencies and organizations
- **Topic 6:** Important central and state government policies and programmes

## Reasoning and Mental Ability

- **Topic 1:** Reasoning and analytical ability
- **Topic 2:** Decision making and problem-solving ability
- **Topic 3:** General mental ability
- **Topic 4:** Basic numerical ability
- **Topic 5:** Data interpretation (charts, graphs, tables, data sufficiency)

## Economy of India and Gujarat

- **Topic 1:** Basic concepts and notions of economics
- **Topic 2:** Indian economy before independence, planning, reforms, NITI Aayog
- **Topic 3:** Agriculture: crops, irrigation, land reforms, pricing, food security, PDS, sustainable and organic farming
- **Topic 4:** Industrial policy, public sector, privatization, SEZ, investment and competition
- **Topic 5:** Infrastructure: water, sanitation, energy, transport, telecom, social impact assessment
- **Topic 6:** Population trends, poverty, inequality, unemployment and development indicators
- **Topic 7:** Public finance: tax, expenditure, debt, deficit, subsidies, fiscal and monetary policy, GST
- **Topic 8:** Foreign trade and balance of payments
- **Topic 9:** Economy of Gujarat: social sectors and development policies

## Geography

- **Topic 1:** General geography: earth, atmosphere, climate, oceans, resources, disasters
- **Topic 2:** Physical geography of world, India and Gujarat
- **Topic 3:** Social geography: population, literacy, migration, urbanization, groups
- **Topic 4:** Economic geography: agriculture, industry, services, transport and trade
- **Topic 5:** Geography in current trends

## Science and Technology

- **Topic 1:** Nature and scope of science and technology, institutions, scientists
- **Topic 2:** ICT, governance, netiquette, cyber security, cyber crime policy
- **Topic 3:** Space and defence technology: ISRO, DRDO, satellite and missile programmes
- **Topic 4:** Energy requirements, resources, deficits and policy
- **Topic 5:** Nuclear policy, cooperation, NSG, non-proliferation
- **Topic 6:** Environmental science, biodiversity, climate change, pollution, legal framework, disaster management
- **Topic 7:** Biotechnology, nanotechnology, emerging technologies and ethics
- **Topic 8:** Science and technology organizations and contributions
